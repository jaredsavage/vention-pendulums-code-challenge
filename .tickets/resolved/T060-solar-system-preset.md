# T060: The solar-system preset

- **Ticket Author:** GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5
- **Resolver:** GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5

Eight planets with their real masses, and radii on a square-root scale so the small planets stay visible.

## How it got there

- Solar system preset: Added eight planets in orbital order with NASA Earth-relative mass and radius ratios, plus optional preset member names.
- Visible planet sizes: The preset's radii now use a square-root scale, 0.15 m × √(Earth-relative radius), instead of a linear 0.1 m × ratio.
- Real planet masses: The preset uses NASA fact-sheet masses, from Mercury at 3.301 × 10²³ kg to Jupiter at 1.898 × 10²⁷ kg.
